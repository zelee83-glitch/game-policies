Privacy Policy

Magic Ball / Merge Drop · Android package: com.wizardacad.magicball

Updated November 20, 2026

1. About the game and its publisher

This draft describes information that may be processed when you use the mobile puzzle game Magic Ball (project name: Merge Drop, the “Game”).

Developer / publisher display name: lee.D (developer-provided; exact Play Console identity has not been independently verified).

Privacy and support email: jakshow@foxmail.com.

Last updated: November 20, 2026. The final policy effective date will be assigned after release review.

Planned availability: worldwide. Intended audience: people aged 13 or older. Country-specific rollout, age categories and advertising/consent requirements are not yet verified.

2. Information involved in gameplay

2.1 Locally stored gameplay data

The inspected Game implementation uses on-device storage for settings (including language and audio preferences), local scores and a top-ten leaderboard, and in-progress game/session snapshots. These are local game features; this does not by itself establish whether data is transmitted by all components included in the release build.

The current Android export configuration disables user-data backup. Actual installed-release backup and deletion behavior still requires device verification.

2.2 Advertising and privacy preferences

The Android project includes Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for banner advertising and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent and privacy choices. The project's release configuration contains advertising identifiers for a production AdMob app and banner unit; whether a specific build serves live ads must be confirmed on devices.

If enabled, Google and advertising partners may process device information, IP addresses, advertising identifiers, ad interactions, diagnostic information, and consent-related signals. The actual categories, purposes, legal basis, partners and sharing depend on the final SDKs, configurations, region, and user choices.

The final ad/consent SDK versions, enabled formats, data categories, service providers and region-specific choices need to be verified against the actual shipping bundle.

2.3 Other services

The inspected game design has no user account or cloud leaderboard requirement, and no purchase or analytics feature was identified in the inspected game documentation. This is not a guarantee that the final app has no additional SDKs, network calls or personal data processing.

A complete audit of the shipping Android bundle for additional SDKs, permissions, analytics, crash reporting and purchase processing is still pending.

2.4 Optional support requests

You can contact jakshow@foxmail.com. We may use the email address, message and voluntarily supplied device details to respond to your request.

The intended support correspondence retention period is 180 days. The cleanup process, any legally required retention exceptions and email-provider handling remain to be verified.

3. Purposes of processing

  • Maintain local game settings, saved progress, and high scores.
  • Where enabled, load and display advertising and manage required privacy choices.
  • Respond to voluntarily submitted support inquiries, if a support service is offered.

Final processing purposes and additional services will be reviewed against the release build and applicable regional rules before this draft is finalized.

4. Third-party services and disclosures

Where active, AdMob and UMP may involve processing by Google and advertising partners. This Game's developer must verify any other processors and what is disclosed or shared before publication.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob privacy and policy guidance

The full list of advertising partners and other processors, sharing arrangements and international transfers is still being verified for release.

5. Retention and deletion

Current Game code saves local progress and settings on a player's device. Clearing app storage or uninstalling may remove these files, subject to actual device, operating-system and backup behavior.

Retention, deletion and request handling for any Google advertising, consent data, optional support messages or other third-party processing must be verified separately.

Game data stored on the device can generally be removed by clearing the app data or uninstalling the game, subject to operating-system behavior. We intend to retain support correspondence for no more than 180 days unless applicable law or an unresolved request requires otherwise; this process still needs operational verification. To request deletion of support correspondence or ask about data handling, contact jakshow@foxmail.com. Third-party advertising or consent data may be subject to separate retention and rights processes.

6. Your choices and rights

The current Game implementation includes a UMP privacy-options entry in the settings interface where the SDK indicates that it is required. Availability depends on the release build, region and applicable rules. Device and Google account settings may offer further controls.

For privacy inquiries and requests to access, correct or delete information or exercise other applicable rights, contact jakshow@foxmail.com. Details of regional rights handling will be reviewed before the final policy is published.

7. Children and age-related settings

The intended audience is ages 13 and older, with worldwide release planned. Regional rules concerning minors, consent, ad settings and any Google Play Families obligations still require review; 13+ is not a verified legal age classification.

8. Security

Current game code stores scores, settings and active sessions locally. Security safeguards for data transmission, SDK processing and developer access will be checked as part of the release review.

9. Changes to this policy

The final policy should reflect the Game's current release and be updated if its data-processing practices change. The preparation date above is not a finalized effective date.
