How it plays

Drop, merge, and chain.

Learn the rules in a moment. Make every drop count as the board fills and your score climbs.

1

Drop an orb

Choose a landing position and release your next orb into the physics-driven play area.

2

Trigger a merge

Matching orbs combine into higher tiers. Quick follow-up merges can build a combo multiplier.

3

Beat your best

Score as much as you can in a 90-second round, and review your locally saved top scores.

Play your way

A little alchemy. A lot of momentum.

The current project features a starry alchemy theme, cascading merge feedback, pause and resume, and local progress saving. Gameplay and visuals may evolve before release.

Android build and ad integration are in testing; public availability has not been confirmed. iOS is a development goal, not a published release.
