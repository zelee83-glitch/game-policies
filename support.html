Common questions

Quick answers

Where can I download BlockPop?

BlockPop is currently in internal testing. A verified official store link will be added when the game is publicly available. We do not provide unofficial APK downloads here.

How do I change sound or other settings?

Open the in-game settings menu to adjust sound, music, and vibration where supported by your installed build.

What if the game does not work as expected?

Try restarting the game and checking your device's available storage. Once the support channel is published, include your game version, Android version, device model, and a short description of the issue. Do not share passwords or payment card numbers.

Can I remove ads or restore a purchase?

Purchase and restore flows are under testing. Availability depends on the installed release and actual Google Play configuration; no live purchase or restore capability is promised yet.

Where is the privacy policy?

See the BlockPop privacy policy draft. Its developer contact and final SDK data disclosures still need confirmation before Play submission.
