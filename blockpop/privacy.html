1. About this policy

This page describes the planned privacy disclosure for the mobile game BlockPop (the “Game”).

Developer / publisher display name: lee.D (provided by the developer; exact Play Console identity requires verification).

Privacy and support email: jakshow@foxmail.com.

Planned distribution: worldwide. Intended audience: ages 13 and older. Country-specific age/consent requirements and actual rollout remain under review.

2. Data and information handled

2.1 Local game information

Current game code maintains gameplay information such as the board, score, high score, piece state, preferences, and game settings in local application storage. Exact Android backup and data-removal behavior for the shipping build needs verification.

2.2 Advertising and consent (conditional on release configuration)

The current development project includes the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent flow. Where these components are enabled, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, diagnostics, and privacy choices, depending on the configuration and the user's region and choices.

Final ad-unit formats, SDK versions, advertising partners, transmitted data, legal basis and consent behavior require verification for the shipping build. Development test ads do not establish production behavior.

2.3 In-app purchases (conditional on release configuration)

The project also includes Google Play Billing integration. If purchases are enabled in the released Game, Google Play handles payment processing and the Game or a verification service may process product identifiers, purchase tokens, purchase status, and entitlement records to deliver or restore paid features.

Whether purchases are active, how purchase tokens are handled and how long transaction or entitlement information is retained must be verified for the final release.

2.4 Support requests

If you contact jakshow@foxmail.com, we may use your message, email address and device or app details you voluntarily provide to respond. We intend to retain support correspondence for up to 180 days; cleanup operations and any lawful exceptions still need verification.

3. How information is used

  • To operate the Game and retain local gameplay progress and preferences.
  • Where advertising is enabled, to request and display ads and manage privacy choices.
  • Where purchases are enabled, to process, validate and restore entitlements.
  • To respond to support inquiries, if a support channel is offered.

The final Android bundle still needs a full data-flow and SDK audit, including any analytics, crash reporting or additional services.

4. Service providers and sharing

Depending on the released Game's enabled services, information may be processed by Google through AdMob, UMP, and Google Play Billing. Additional advertising partners or processors must be identified from the deployed configuration, rather than assumed to be absent.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob policies and privacy guidance

Any additional service providers, data sharing and cross-border processing will be verified against the shipping build before publication of the final policy.

5. Data storage, retention and deletion

Gameplay settings and score data are stored on the device by the current Game code. Removing the app's local data may remove this information, subject to any operating-system backup or restore behavior.

Retention and deletion for ads, purchases, consent signals, support correspondence, and any server-side data depend on the services used in the final build.

Clearing app data or uninstalling generally removes on-device game information, subject to Android backup and restore behavior. We intend to retain support correspondence for no more than 180 days unless a legal exception or outstanding inquiry requires longer, but this process must be verified. For support correspondence deletion and data inquiries, contact jakshow@foxmail.com. Ad, consent and purchase services may follow separate retention and request processes.

6. Your choices and rights

The current advertising integration includes UMP privacy-choice handling where required by configuration and applicable law. Availability of the choice interface varies by region and the released build. Users may also control some advertising preferences through Android or Google settings.

For privacy inquiries, access, correction, deletion, objection and other applicable requests, contact jakshow@foxmail.com. Country-specific rights and response procedures still require release review.

7. Children and age requirements

The developer intends BlockPop for users aged 13 and older and plans worldwide distribution. Regional rules for children and teens, appropriate advertising/consent controls and Google Play Families applicability have not yet been fully reviewed.

8. Security

Game scores and settings are stored locally by the current implementation. The final build's safeguards for transmission, third-party SDK processing and access to support information remain under review.

9. Policy updates

The policy may be revised as the Game and its services change. The date shown above identifies this draft; future finalized revisions should show an effective date and accurately describe the released version.
